Wadhakkir Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Who we are

Wadhakkir (وذكّر, Google Play com.alfehras.wadhakkir) is developed and published by Elevenist.dev, the developer name shown on Google Play. That developer account belongs to Yahya Al-Titi, owner of AL TITI ESTABLISHMENT FOR SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGY, Amman, Jordan. In this policy, “we” means the developer.

In short

No accounts and no ads. The app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can see which reminders and features help; they carry no name, no advertising ID, never your location and never what you recite. We never sell or share any data for advertising.

Location

Wadhakkir asks for your approximate location to work out prayer times, the last third of the night and the other moments of your day. The calculation happens on your phone. Your location is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; their text and times are never sent to a server. When you open a reminder, only its kind (for example "morning adhkar") is counted in the usage statistics below.

The daily Mushaf page

To show a printed page of the Quran each day, the app downloads the page image from our server, api.wadhakkir.alfehras.com, or, if it is unavailable, from files.quran.app. The request contains only the page number. Downloaded pages are kept on your phone.

Usage statistics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how it is used: for example app opens, which tab or adhkar category is opened, which kind of reminder is opened, which moment is shown, completed categories and moments marked done, tasbih rounds, fasting days viewed, opening a page or verse in Rattil Quran, and setting changes (such as the language or a reminder switched off; for your location and home only that they were set, never where). Firebase also records general device information (model, operating system, app version, language and approximate country) and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase. We do not collect the advertising ID, do not allow ad personalisation, and never send your location or the text of any dhikr. Google processes this data for us under its Firebase privacy terms; it is kept for up to 14 months. Uninstalling the app resets the identifier.

Links to other apps

When our mushaf app, Rattil Quran, is installed, Wadhakkir can open it at a page or verse, and Rattil Quran can open Wadhakkir at a chosen screen. These links carry only the screen, page or verse to open.

Technical logs

Like any web server, ours keeps short technical logs (IP address, time and the address requested) for security and troubleshooting. They are not used to identify you and are not shared.

Your progress and settings

What you mark as done, your tasbih counts, your language and your reminder settings are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Contacting us

If you report an error or write to us from the app, it opens your own email app with a message to support@alfehras.com that includes your phone's operating system and version, the app's language and, when you report an item, its id, so we can reproduce the problem. You see the message and decide whether to send it. We use your email only to reply.

Children

Wadhakkir is suitable for the whole family and collects no personal information from anyone, including children, beyond the anonymous usage statistics described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Elevenist.dev (Yahya Al-Titi, AL TITI ESTABLISHMENT FOR SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGY), Amman, Jordan · support@alfehras.com

باختصار

صفحة المصحف اليومية

لعرض صفحة من المصحف المطبوع كل يوم، ينزّل التطبيق صورة الصفحة من خادمنا api.wadhakkir.alfehras.com، أو من files.quran.app إن تعذّر خادمنا. ولا يحمل الطلب إلا رقم الصفحة، وتُحفظ الصفحات المنزّلة على هاتفك.

إحصاءات الاستخدام

يستخدم التطبيق Google Firebase Analytics لإحصاء طريقة استخدامه، مثل: مرات فتح التطبيق، والتبويب أو باب الأذكار الذي يُفتح، ونوع التذكير الذي يُفتح، واللحظة المعروضة، والأبواب المكتملة واللحظات المعلَّمة منجزة، ودورات التسبيح، وأيام الصيام التي تُعرض، وفتح صفحة أو آية في «رتّل القرآن»، وتغيير الإعدادات (مثل اللغة أو إيقاف تذكير؛ أما موقعك وبيتك فلا يُحصى إلا أنهما ضُبطا، لا مكانهما). ويسجّل Firebase كذلك معلومات عامة عن الجهاز (الطراز ونظام التشغيل وإصدار التطبيق واللغة والبلد التقريبي) ومعرّفًا عشوائيًا للتطبيق ينشئه Firebase. لا نجمع المعرّف الإعلاني، ولا نسمح بتخصيص الإعلانات، ولا نرسل موقعك ولا نص أي ذكر أبدًا. تعالج Google هذه البيانات لصالحنا وفق شروط الخصوصية في Firebase، وتُحفظ مدة أقصاها ١٤ شهرًا. وحذف التطبيق يعيد تعيين المعرّف.

الروابط إلى التطبيقات الأخرى

إذا كان تطبيقنا للمصحف «رتّل القرآن» مثبّتًا، يستطيع «وذكّر» أن يفتحه على صفحة أو آية، ويستطيع «رتّل القرآن» أن يفتح «وذكّر» على شاشة بعينها. ولا تحمل هذه الروابط إلا الشاشة أو الصفحة أو الآية المطلوبة.
