What we collect

No personal information. The apps do not collect, store or share any personal information, and there are no accounts and no ads.

Usage statistics

All the apps listed above use Google Firebase Analytics, from these versions on: Learn Hebrew Letters 3.0.1, Hebrew Words 1.0.1, Osmanlıca Harfleri Öğren 1.0.1, Learn Russian Letters & Sounds 1.0.1, Learn Turkish 3.0.1, Turkish Numbers & Time 2.0.1, Turkish Food & Restaurants 2.0.1, Turkish Travel Phrases 2.0.1 and Osmanlıca Kelimeler 1.0.2. They use it to count how they are used: which lessons are started, finished or left early (with the stars and first-try answers of a finished lesson), practice sessions, language changes and opening the privacy note. Firebase also records general device information (model, operating system, app version, language and approximate country) and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase. We do not collect the advertising ID, do not allow ad personalisation, and never send what you type or answer. Google processes this data for us under its Firebase privacy terms; it is kept for up to 14 months. Uninstalling the app resets the identifier.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, stars, streak and chosen language are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, apart from the anonymous counts described above. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet

The apps work fully offline. All lessons, sounds and pictures are inside the app. Apps with usage statistics send them when a connection is available.

Children

No personal information is collected from anyone, including children; the only data sent is the anonymous usage statistics described above. The apps are suitable for learners of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Google bu verileri bizim adımıza Firebase gizlilik koşullarına göre işler ve en fazla 14 ay saklar. Uygulamayı silmek kimliği sıfırlar.
