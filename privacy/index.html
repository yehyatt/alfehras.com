Who we are

The Alfehras apps are developed and published by AppSpark9, the developer name shown on Google Play. The AppSpark9 developer account on Google Play is registered to Yahya Al-Titi, owner of AL TITI ESTABLISHMENT FOR SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGY, and AppSpark9 is a trade name of that establishment, Amman, Jordan. On the App Store they are sold by Yehya AlTiti. Alfehras is the name of the app family. In this policy, “we” means AppSpark9.

Apps covered by this policy

  • Learn Hebrew Letters (تعلّم الحروف العبرية): Google Play com.HebrewAlphabetQuiz; App Store com.alfehras.hebrew
  • Osmanlıca Harfleri Öğren, also listed as Ottoman Letters: Elifba (تعلّم قراءة الحروف العثمانية): Google Play and App Store com.alfehras.ottoman
  • Learn Russian Letters & Sounds (تعلّم الحروف الروسية): Google Play com.RussianAlphabetQuiz; App Store com.alfehras.russian
  • Learn Turkish: Letters & Words (تعلّم التركية: حروف وكلمات): Google Play com.TurkishArabicQuizOne; App Store com.alfehras.turkish
  • Turkish Numbers & Time, until its update currently listed on Google Play as Turkish Numbers Quiz (الأرقام والوقت بالتركية): Google Play com.TurkishNumbersQuiz; App Store com.alfehras.turkishnumbers
  • Turkish Food & Restaurants, until its update currently listed on Google Play as Turkish Food Names Quiz (التركية للطعام والمطاعم): Google Play com.TurkishFoodsQuiz; App Store com.alfehras.turkishfood
  • Turkish Travel Phrases, also listed as Turkish Phrases for Travel, and until its update currently listed on Google Play as Turkish Colors Quiz (عبارات السفر بالتركية): Google Play com.TurkishColorsQuiz; App Store com.alfehras.turkishtravel
  • علم الكلمات العبريه ١ (Hebrew Words): Google Play com.HebrewWordsQuiz

What we collect

Nothing. The apps do not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, stars, streak and chosen language are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet

The apps work fully offline. All lessons, sounds and pictures are inside the app.

Children

Because no data is collected, the apps are safe for learners of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
